# Monsoon Mango Mission — Social Video Season Kit

**Review status:** Draft for owner review. Nothing is scheduled or published.  
**Format:** 10 original 40-second vertical animated episodes for Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels.  
**Series setting:** Neelvan, a fictional village near a mango grove and rain-fed stream.

## Original cast and visual rules

- **Aru:** brave, athletic village kid; teal vest, orange wristbands, short dark hair. Learns to pause and plan.
- **Mira:** observant planner; purple rain jacket, braided hair, small field notebook.
- **Kittu:** curious inventor; yellow poncho, round glasses, handmade everyday-material gadgets.
- **Tara:** expressive squirrel scout; tiny blue scarf, communicates with gestures and squeaks.

Use original storybook animation, saturated monsoon greens, mango yellow, teal and violet. Keep cast designs and voices consistent. Do not use, mention, imitate, or visually resemble Chhota Bheem or any other existing franchise. No copied costumes, catchphrases, logos, characters, or music. Do not depict dangerous stunts or children entering rushing water. Check name and trademark availability before adopting the working title commercially.

## Free-first workflow

1. Paste one episode brief into InVideo, using the series prompt template. Use free access/credits only and check current export limits, watermark rules, and commercial-use terms.
2. Review the complete output for character consistency, narration, captions, music/asset rights, framing, and duration. Revise errors before approval.
3. Export as `MMM_E01_v1.mp4`, etc. Keep a note of the source and rights for every sound/image/clip.
4. Schedule the approved file in Meta Business Suite for connected Facebook and Instagram accounts if that option is available; otherwise post manually. Keep approval before publishing.
5. Track status, scheduled/published date, URL, views, watch time, comments, and lessons.

Prompts and copy can be prepared without charge; automatic generation/export and automatic publishing may depend on account features, paid plans, and Meta permissions. Never put account passwords or API tokens in this repository.
